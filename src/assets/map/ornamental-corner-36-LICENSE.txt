Design 36 (row 5, column 4).
Adaptation: 145 original CAD segments joined into 6 closed vector contours,
reflected, scaled, recolored bronze, and mirrored with continuous connecting rails.
Finish: soft bronze gradient, fine directional edge highlights, sparse vector
wear marks, and a narrow contact shadow. Lighting paths omit terminal edges
to keep corner-to-rail joins smooth. No procedural noise or brushed texture.
Added paired geometric motifs along both edges of a continuous decorative band.
Central raised plaques carry folded copper-band knots, with open crossings,
elongated angular openwork, diamond terminals, and restrained edge lighting.
The patterned band continues through mitered diagonal corner fields beneath
the original corner metalwork, with separate horizontal/diagonal/vertical directions.
