## 中文

[placements.json](placements.json) 记录航船位置，`ship-*.webp` 为航船图像。渲染入口是 [MapShips.jsx](../../src/studies/MapShips.jsx)。移动航船后，在地图上检查航线附近的对齐和缩放效果。

## English

[Read in Simplified Chinese / 简体中文](#中文)

[placements.json](placements.json) records ship positions, and `ship-*.webp` contains ship artwork. [MapShips.jsx](../../src/studies/MapShips.jsx) renders the overlay. After moving a ship, check alignment with nearby routes across zoom levels.
